## Sound

Narration: Kokoro `af_heart`, synthesized locally at 1.02 speed. Explainroo's Whisper alignment confirmed 100% of the script's spoken words. Cues use actual word timestamps and script markers, not approximate word durations.

Music: original 48 kHz stereo composition synthesized in `compose.py`, with open extended harmonies, felt-key-like tones, a low pulse, stereo delays, soft air transitions and a final F-major resolution. Explainroo normalizes and ducks the score beneath the voice. Additional transition, notification and brand sound effects are generated locally.

## Product grounding

The product story is based on the repository README, `ui/src/App.tsx`, and the detector/alert/analytics architecture. No accuracy, latency, clinical certification, or guaranteed outcome is asserted. The person, phone, event history and metrics are visual illustrations, not captured production data. The ending identifies SafeStep as a research prototype.

## Toolkit

[Explainroo](https://github.com/vincentsch/explainroo) provides local speech, Whisper timing, deterministic browser Canvas rendering, FFmpeg encoding, audio finishing, subtitles and validation. The visual compositions and original score were authored specifically for this film.
